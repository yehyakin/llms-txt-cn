Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-01

We collect as little as we can. Running a check needs no account, sets no cookies and uses no analytics or ad trackers.

When you check a domain

  • Our servers request public files from that domain: robots.txt, llms.txt and the homepage. Those requests come from us, not from your browser.
  • We don't store the domains you check or your IP address. Your IP address is used for a moment to rate-limit requests, then discarded.
  • Each result is cached for about five minutes, keyed by the domain, so repeat checks don't hit the site again. Share images for reports are cached for up to an hour.

When you join the early-access list or contact us

  • We store the email address, plan and site you enter, the message if you send one, and the country your request came from (as reported by our hosting provider). We don't store your IP address.
  • We use this only to tell you when your account is ready and to reply to you. We don't sell it or share it for marketing.
  • To have your details deleted, send a request through the contact form from the same email address. We delete it within 30 days.

When you turn on monitoring

  • We store your email address, the site you asked us to watch, your language, and a short summary of each re-test: the three grades and which AI crawlers were blocked. We don't store your IP address.
  • Monitoring starts only after you click the link in the confirmation email. We use the email address only to send alerts about that site.
  • Every alert has a stop link. Stopping deletes the site, its summaries and the link between them and your email straight away.
  • Alert emails are sent through Resend.

Service providers

The site runs on Cloudflare (hosting, caching and storage). Some checks are sent through our own server when a site blocks Cloudflare's network. Cloudflare processes requests under its privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If we start collecting something new, we'll say so here first.
