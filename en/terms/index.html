Terms of use

Last updated 2026-11-03

Using the checks

  • Check sites you own, manage, or have a legitimate reason to look at. Don't use VAKSEL to load-test, scan or attack a site.
  • Checks are rate-limited. Don't try to get around the limits with automation or multiple networks.

What the results mean

Results are our best reading at the time of the check. We test from data-center IP addresses with each crawler's user agent, not from the crawlers' own networks, so a site that verifies crawler IP addresses may treat the real crawler differently. Each result shows a confidence level for this reason. Crawler descriptions follow each operator's public documentation as of the date shown.
