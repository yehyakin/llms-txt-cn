Questions

What does monitoring check?

The same three layers as the free check (robots.txt, live requests as each AI search crawler with control groups, and page readability) on a daily schedule. You get an email when a result changes, for example when a CDN update starts blocking OAI-SearchBot.

When does it launch?

Monitoring is in early access. Join the list and we will email you when your account is ready. You pay nothing until then.

Why would my site start blocking AI crawlers by itself?

CDN defaults change (Cloudflare changed its AI bot controls on September 15, 2026), security plugins update their bot lists, and robots.txt gets edited during redesigns. None of these tell you.

Do you test from the crawlers’ real IP addresses?

No one outside those companies can. We test from a data center with each crawler’s user agent and compare against a normal browser, Googlebot and an unknown bot. That tells a targeted AI block apart from a general bot-verification rule, and the report shows the confidence for each result.
