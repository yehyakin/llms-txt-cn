KimiBot

Operator月之暗面 Kimi
PurposeTraining
Follows robots.txtYes
SourceOfficial docs ↗

Kimi's training crawler. Moonshot says disallowing it signals your content shouldn't be used to train its foundation models.

What happens if you block it

Only opts you out of training. Moonshot doesn't say it affects Kimi search.

Our take

Block it if you don't want your content in Kimi's training data.

robots.txt

To block it:

User-agent: KimiBot
Disallow: /

To allow it explicitly (useful when a wider rule blocks other bots):

User-agent: KimiBot
Allow: /
