ByteDance's crawler, documented as the user agent for Toutiao search.

What happens if you block it

Your site may drop out of Toutiao search. ByteDance doesn't document whether blocking it stops AI training use.

Our take

Block it if you don't need Toutiao traffic. Its training use is undocumented.

robots.txt

To block it:

User-agent: Bytespider
Disallow: /

To allow it explicitly (useful when a wider rule blocks other bots):

User-agent: Bytespider
Allow: /
