GPTBot

OperatorOpenAI
PurposeTraining
Follows robots.txtYes
SourceOfficial docs ↗

OpenAI's training crawler. It collects public pages that may be used to train OpenAI's generative foundation models.

What happens if you block it

Your content is excluded from training OpenAI's foundation models. It does not affect whether you appear in ChatGPT search; that is OAI-SearchBot.

Our take

Block it if you don't want your content used for training. Blocking it alone won't remove you from ChatGPT answers.

robots.txt

To block it:

User-agent: GPTBot
Disallow: /

To allow it explicitly (useful when a wider rule blocks other bots):

User-agent: GPTBot
Allow: /

The operator's own words

Disallowing GPTBot indicates a site's content should not be used in training generative AI foundation models.OpenAI documentation, checked 2026-10-31
