AI crawler directory

Search, training and user-triggered crawlers are separate agents. Blocking the wrong one can take you out of AI answers without stopping training. Every entry links the operator's own documentation.

User-triggered fetchers

Fetches a page in real time because a user asked the assistant to.

ChatGPT-User
OpenAI

Fetches made when a ChatGPT or Custom GPT user asks it to visit a page.

Claude-User
Anthropic

Fetches made when a Claude user asks a question that needs your page.

Perplexity-User
Perplexity

Fetches made when a Perplexity user asks a question that needs your page.

meta-externalfetcher
Meta

Fetches requested by Meta AI users, for example when a user shares a link.

Kimi-User
Moonshot AI (Kimi)

Fetches pages when a Kimi user asks it to read a page or answer with live web results.

MistralAI-User
Mistral AI

Fetches a page when someone asks Mistral's assistant about it, and links the page as a source.

Amzn-User
Amazon

Amazon's user-triggered fetcher.

Training crawlers

Collects content that may be used to train AI models.

GPTBot
OpenAI

OpenAI's training crawler.

ClaudeBot
Anthropic

Anthropic's training crawler.

meta-externalagent
Meta

Meta's crawler for use cases such as training foundation AI models or improving products by indexing content directly.

Amazonbot
Amazon

Amazon's crawler, used to improve Amazon products and services.

KimiBot
Moonshot AI (Kimi)

Kimi's training crawler.

MistralAI-Training
Mistral AI

Collects web content to build datasets for training Mistral's generative AI models.

Training control tokens

A robots.txt token that controls AI use of content another crawler fetched. It never fetches pages itself.

Google-Extended
Google

A robots.txt product token, not a separate crawler.

Applebot-Extended
Apple

A robots.txt token, not a crawler.

Open datasets

Builds an open dataset that third parties use, including for AI training.

CCBot
Common Crawl

Common Crawl's crawler.

Other AI agents

Fetches pages for a specific product, such as ad review or building an agent on your own site. Not search, not training.

OAI-AdsBot
OpenAI

OpenAI's ad review agent.

Google-CloudVertexBot
Google

Google's crawler for Vertex AI Agents.
