meta-externalagent

OperatorMeta
PurposeTraining

Meta's crawler for use cases such as training foundation AI models or improving products by indexing content directly.

What happens if you block it

Meta-ExternalAgent stops fetching your content, including for training Meta's foundation models.

Our take

Block it to opt out of Meta AI training.

robots.txt

To block it:

User-agent: meta-externalagent
Disallow: /

To allow it explicitly (useful when a wider rule blocks other bots):

User-agent: meta-externalagent
Allow: /
